02 / THE DECISION

We pick REST.

For a Student Management System, boring is beautiful. REST gives students, staff and third-party tools a shared language they already understand: HTTP.

“

Good architecture makes the right thing feel obvious.

— GZU engineering notes
01

Everyone speaks HTTP

Browsers, mobile apps and campus integrations can jump in without a specialist client.

02

Clear resources

Students, courses and enrollments map cleanly to URLs and verbs: GET, POST, PATCH, DELETE.

03

Easy to debug

Readable JSON plus familiar status codes make testing in Postman or a browser a breeze.

04

Grows with the campus

Stateless requests, caching and versioned endpoints keep future releases flexible.
